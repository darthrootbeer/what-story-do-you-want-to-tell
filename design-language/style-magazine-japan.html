01 Introduction
序文
The Design
of Storytelling 物語の設計

Storyteller is a voice-first, AI-driven interactive narrative platform. Before it is a product, it is a typographic argument: that the structure of information — the grid on which choices, consequences, and characters are arranged — is inseparable from the experience of story itself. Müller-Brockmann called this integration of structure and content the basis of all serious design. We apply the same logic to interactive fiction.

The Swiss International Typographic Style, codified in the 1950s and still the invisible grammar of professional print, rests on four premises: mathematical grid, objective photography, sans-serif typography, and logical hierarchy. Storyteller's interaction design inherits these premises and extends them into time. The grid is not the page — it is the story engine. The hierarchy is not headings — it is the branching structure of choice.

This annual examines Storyteller as a designed object. Not a product. A designed object. The same critical attention given to a Fuse poster or an early Helvetica specimen is applied here to interaction architecture, typographic voice, and the grammar of an AI narrator.

We invite disagreement. Serious design invites disagreement. The only unacceptable position is indifference to the arrangement of things.

01 / Publication note

This document is a typographic specimen as much as a design critique. The layout IS the argument. Read the grid before reading the text.

02 / Reference

Josef Müller-Brockmann, Grid Systems in Graphic Design, Niggli, 1981. The structural logic of this page descends directly from Chapter 4.

03 / Typeface note

Latin text: Helvetica Neue (system). Japanese text: Noto Serif JP, weights 300 and 900. The weight contrast is intentional — it is the visual counterpoint, not decoration.

On the full-bleed statement In Swiss typography, the typographic moment is a single phrase set at maximum scale, bleeding beyond the grid. It is not a headline. It is a structural claim. Read it as you would read a load-bearing wall — not for decoration but for what it holds up.

The System システム

Design systems are not style guides. A style guide describes surfaces. A design system describes relationships. Storyteller's system describes the relationship between voice, structure, and time — the three variables that determine whether a story feels real.

01 / The Narrator 語り手 katarite Primary narrative voice. Always present, never breaks scene. Owns tone, pacing, and continuity.
02 / The Guide ガイド gaido Human interface for corrections. Operates between scenes. Never interrupts Storytime.
03 / The Play Engine プレイエンジン pure-i-enjin Rules interpreter and consequence logic. Handles dice, probability, and mechanical outcomes.
04 / TOME Format 形式 keishiki Story Package specification. Canonical format for authored narrative content and world-state data.
05 / The Chronicle 年代記 nendaiki Persistent memory layer. Logs choices, consequences, and character arcs across sessions.
06 / Storytime 物語の時間 monogatari no jikan The active narrative experience. The state in which no mechanical interruptions are permitted.
