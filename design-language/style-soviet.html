Принципы — Principles

The collective voice
is louder than the author's.

  • 1
    The Author Is Dead.

    Every story that has ever been told required a listener. The listener is now a participant. The author recedes the moment the words leave their mouth. What remains is yours.

  • 2
    The Machine Serves the People.

    Storyteller does not generate content. It generates conditions for human experience. The tool does not speak. You speak. The tool clears the room.

  • 3
    All Stories Are Political.

    The choice of what story gets told, and how, is never neutral. Every form is an argument. We choose openness. We choose the reader over the institution.

  • 4
    History Belongs to Those Who Live It.

    Your version of the story is the correct one. Not because it is accurate. Because it is yours, and you carried it here, and no archive gets to tell you otherwise.

She had walked this road many times, always in the same direction, always carrying the same weight. But today something had shifted in the marrow of the morning — a word had been spoken, and words, once spoken, have a way of becoming walls.

The word had been: remember. Someone had said it quietly, standing close. As if remembering were something you could instruct a person to do. As if the past were a file that could be opened and closed. The story remembered anyway. Every witness who told it in a kitchen, in a car, in a field — each one an act of defiance against the official silence.

The story remembers everything the state forgets. Every whispered name, every burned document — the story holds it. Not as evidence. Not as accusation. As the simple, absolute fact of what occurred, which does not require permission to remain true.

Design Rationale

Form is function.
Function is power.

Soviet Constructivism understood something about design that most movements miss: the form is the argument. The size of the type is not aesthetic preference — it is a claim about importance. The angle of the composition is not decoration — it is urgency made visible.

This is why Constructivist design still lands. It does not ask you to admire it. It demands that you respond to it. Every element takes a position. Ambiguity is, in Rodchenko's famous formulation, a bourgeois luxury.

Typography as Weapon

Constructivism understood that type was not decoration — it was argumentation. Size, weight, placement are rhetoric. Every typographic choice is a political choice.

Colour as Ideology

Red and black eliminate the middle ground. Every element takes a position. Ambiguity is a bourgeois luxury. The cream is newsprint — the surface where truth gets pressed.

The Reader as Comrade

Soviet design assumed the reader was capable of confronting big ideas in bold form. It treated attention as something to be earned, not coddled. So does Storyteller.
